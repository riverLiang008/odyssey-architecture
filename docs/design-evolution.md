# Design Evolution

Odyssey’s design did not arrive as a finished system. Its central ideas became clearer through visual experiments, structural revisions, and repeated choices about what to preserve.

These stories document that process. They are not a version history or a claim that every decision followed a straight line. They show how an abstract design concern became a more explicit visual and structural language.
